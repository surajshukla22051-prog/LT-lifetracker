LifeTrack

Build your life.

A simple space for daily progress

LifeTrack helps you build routines and review your personal progress. Track daily habits, goals, reading, focus sessions, and wellness entries such as steps, water, sleep, and mood.

Google sign-in can back up your LifeTrack entries in the private app-data storage of your Google Drive account for use across your devices. If you choose to connect Google Fit or Google Health, LifeTrack reads activity and sleep summaries to show them in Fitness Today; it does not change your health records.
