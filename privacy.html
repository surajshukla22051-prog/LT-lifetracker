LifeTrack Privacy Policy

Made by Suraj Shukla

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what LifeTrack processes when you use its habit tracker, Google sign-in, Google Drive backup, and optional Google Fit or Google Health connection.

Information LifeTrack processes

  • Google sign-in provides your name, email address, profile photo, and account identifier so LifeTrack can identify your account.
  • Tracker information can include habits, check-ins, goals, reading progress, fitness entries, and body-profile details that you enter.
  • If you authorize Google Fit or Google Health, LifeTrack requests read-only activity and sleep data to display steps and sleep in Fitness Today. LifeTrack uses one health provider at a time, according to its configuration.

How information is stored

LifeTrack stores tracker information in this browser. For Google sign-in accounts, it also keeps a LifeTrack backup in the private Google Drive app-data area of that same Google account to support syncing across devices. Name/email and Guest accounts stay in the current browser and do not sync across devices.

Google services and sharing

Google sign-in, Google Drive backup, and optional Google Fit or Google Health access use Google services directly. Health access is read-only. LifeTrack does not operate a separate server to collect this information and does not sell it or share it with advertisers. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Tokens and permissions

Google access tokens are held in temporary browser session storage and expire. Your LifeTrack account remains signed in, and you can reconnect Google from the dashboard profile menu. You can stop future Google access through your Google Account security settings. Information already stored in this browser or in Google Drive may need to be removed separately.

Data control and contact

You can clear browser-stored LifeTrack data from the app or your browser settings. To ask a question about this policy or request help with data stored in Google Drive, contact surajshukla22051@gmail.com.
